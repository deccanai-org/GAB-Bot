QM1. What are QMs and CBs?
QMs (Queue Managers) bring in and manage contributors (CBs): their availability, performance and training. QMs perform super QC of their CBs' work and escalate blockers.

QM2. Quality control during a batch: 1 in 5
During the batch, QMs QC 1 out of every 5 tasks each CB submits (20% of each CB's work). For every 5 tasks a CB submits, the QM picks 1 at random, gives feedback and decides task allocation using the QM Audit template. The number of CBs a QM handles depends on how many tasks that QM can QC.

QM3. Training before every batch
No CB starts a batch without attending its training, including CBs who worked on earlier batches. QMs should highlight edge cases they notice across tasks and CB questions.

QM4. Onboarding steps
1. Identify QMs and introduce the project: QMs are chosen for language, staffing needs and availability. The project is explained end to end (scope, language, volume, timelines, CB headcount, hours to commit) in a sheet shared with the delivery team. QMs get the project brief, instructions, examples and documents, and a playground with a familiarisation activity. QMs are expected to bring in CBs, manage their availability and performance, perform super QC of their work, and escalate blockers. A Deccan email ID is created for each CB, and they are added to the project Google Chat space (https://chat.google.com/app/chat/AAQAhXgZd8Q).
2. Project introduction call and Cosmo demo: introduce QMs to the project team, clarify responsibilities, set up communication and escalation channels, demo the QM workflows in Cosmo (task access, submission, quality review), answer questions.
3. Confirm the CB roster and share bandwidth with Ops: each QM lists their CBs in one shared roster so kickoff, training and tasking can be scheduled.
4. Complete accounts and verify access: every QM and CB needs an account on deccanexperts.ai (reuse existing ones). QMs' Deccan email IDs go to the access owner for project, document, Cosmo and QM/admin permissions.

QM5. What goes in the shared roster?
- QM details: name, Deccan email ID, platform account email if different.
- CB details: name, registered account email, assigned QM, project language.
- Availability: work date, time zone, local start and end times, and the matching IST window (record a local window and time zone, not a snapshot of current local time).
- Committed capacity: hours committed per work date, and explicit availability for the next day's Phase 5 work.
- Onboarding progress: account and access status, playground status, training slot and completion, readiness status.
- Follow-up: blocker, action owner, expected resolution time.

QM6. When is a contributor "Ready"?
Check in the shared tracker that: the QM completed the project introduction and Cosmo demo; each QM has done one QC task; QM and CB accounts are active and access has been tested; required training and practice are complete, with the project team's readiness confirmation recorded.

QM7. QM responsibilities after deployment
Confirm CB attendance and flag changes to committed hours or staffing to Ops; train CBs and manage task allocation and completion; perform super QC using the agreed method and coverage; give actionable feedback, follow up on corrections, and escalate repeated quality issues or task blockers.
