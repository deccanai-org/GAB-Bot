SR0. Why is a screen recording no longer needed?
The production update effective 13 Nov 2026 and the Phase 6.1 annotation FAQ dated 14 Nov explicitly remove the screen-recording requirement. Debug info is mandatory instead. The PDFs retain older recording guidance and examples, which do not apply to current Phase 6.1 submissions.

SR1. Do I need to record the screen or use OBS for Phase 6.1?
No. A screen recording is not required for Phase 6.1. Submit the complete conversation JSON for Model A and the mandatory Gemini debug JSON. Do not follow older recording pages as a current submission requirement.

SR2. Does the older recording checklist mean I should reject a Phase 6.1 task without a video?
No. Use the dated Phase 6.1 requirement: no screen recording needed. The supplied QC FAQ recording rules are labelled Phase 5. Check Gemini debug, conversation completeness and the required final artefacts instead, within your stage's review scope.
